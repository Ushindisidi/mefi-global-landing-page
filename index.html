How it works

From M-Pesa SMS to verified score in 30 seconds

The MEFI Treasurer Terminal fits into any SACCO's weekly meeting. No new devices, no IT setup, no training required.

01

Member shares their SMS

The trader forwards their M-Pesa transaction confirmations to the SACCO treasurer on WhatsApp. That is the only thing they need to do.

02

Treasurer scores in the app

The treasurer pastes the messages into MEFI on their phone. The app calculates a Reliability Index based on velocity, stability and longevity of trading activity.

03

Signed result is produced

MEFI returns a cryptographically signed score that cannot be faked or altered. The SACCO keeps a full audit trail. The trader has a record they can build on.

Why people trust MEFI

Secure, honest and built around how Kenya actually works

No personal data stored

Counterparty names are hashed before processing. Raw SMS messages are never written to disk. MEFI sees patterns, not people.

Every score is tamper-proof

Results are signed with Ed25519 cryptography. If anyone tries to alter a score, the signature immediately becomes invalid.

No app to install

The Treasurer Terminal runs in any phone browser. No Play Store, no installation, no extra data costs for the treasurer or the member.

Built for real M-Pesa usage

MEFI handles TILL payments, Paybill, direct sends, Fuliza and bank deposits. Built around how Kenyans actually use mobile money.

ODPC compliant by design

Consent is captured before any data is processed. The product was built with Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 in mind from day one.

Works offline

Scores are calculated locally and queued if there is no internet. Data syncs automatically when the connection returns.
